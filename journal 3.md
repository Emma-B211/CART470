Tower-Building Jenga

Reason:

Players want to see how tall they can make the tower.
Cooperation encourages players to communicate and help each other
Players share a goal, so everyone contributes to the same result
A collapsing tower creates unexpected movements
Players can try again to beat their previous height.
Ideas and Suggestions

Different block shapes and sizes
Blocks could have different weights
Random events could shake or move the tower
Players could have random abilities, such as 
Push
Jump
Pick up
Carefully place blocks
Add height milestones or visual changes as the tower grows
A time limit to create pressure
Different tower layout could make some section harder to build
Pros & Cons

Pros:

Easy to understand objective
Cooperative, so theres no elimination
Physical phone controls fit naturally with pushing/placing
The tower creates an obvious representation of progress
Collapses can create exciting moments
Cons:

Players might become frustrated if one person ruins the tower
Building can become repetitive
Players may not have much to do while waiting for their turn
If the tower becomes too difficult, players may stop trying
How to solve the cons

Give every player something to do instead of waiting
Add different block types and challenges
Give players limited control over how strongly they push
Add a safe zone or forgiving physics around the tower
Give players individual mini goals while maintaining the team goal
What will be lost with the pros

If the game becomes too controlled, the chaos of Tower-Building Jenga could disappear.





Air Hockey/Big Soccer Ball

Reason:

Players want to score the most goals than the other team
Competition creates a reason to keep playing
Players can work together with teammates
Players can try different strategies
Attack
Defend
Block
Pass
The giant puck creates unpredictable situations
A close score keeps players invested until the end.
Players can improve their pushing and positioning skills
Ideas and Suggestions

Different ball size
Different arena layouts
Increase amount of ball on the field
Temporary power ups
Goals could move or change size
Different pushing strength
Special rounds such as:
Giant puck
Tiny puck
Multiple puck
Teams could switch sides between rounds
Pros & Cons

Pros:

Very easy to understand: push the puck into the other teams goal
Competitive games naturally encourages play
Everyone can participate simultaneously.
Large numbers of players can potentially join
Cons:

Players may feel useless if they are far away from the ball
Large teams could become too chaotic
One player might dominate the puck
Players could become frustrated if they can’t control the puck.
The game could be repetitive if every round is just push the puck → score
How to solve the cons

Give players different positions or roles
Add a limit to how many players can push the ball at once (unless its just 2 people)
Give players temporary abilities to assist them on trying to win against their opponent.
Make the puck bounce or react differently depending on where it is hit
Add different arena obstacles
Use short rounds so losing doesn’t feel too punishing.
What will be lost with the pros

If there are too many abilities and rules, the simplicity of air hockey/soccer could disappear.

If there are too many roles, power-ups, and complicated rules, players might spend more time learning the game, than actually playing



Maze Game

Reason:

Players want to collect as many gems as possible
Players want to explore the maze and find new paths
The changing maze prevents players from memorizing the map
Players can compete for high scores
Players can race each other to gather as much of the gems as possible.
Players want to see what happens when the maze changes.
Ideas and Suggestions

Different gem values
Rare gems that appear temporarily
Players can steal/collected contested gems
Moving walls
Different maze size
Players could leave temporary paths or markers
Pros & Cons

Pros:

Exploration gives players reason to keep moving
Changing environments incentives players to refamiliarize their new surroundings.
Individual characters make it easy to see what each player is doing
Gem collection provides a clear reward
Cons:

Players could get lost or confused
Players might spend too much time searching without finding anything
Changing the maze too frequently could feel unfair
Players may focus only on collecting gems and ignore the changing environment
Players could get stuck
How to solve the cons

Provide a small map or directional indicator
Highlight nearby gems
Give players a few seconds of warning before the maze changes
Only change specific sections rather than the entire maze
Add checkpoints or safe areas
Give players temporary abilities to escape difficult areas
What will be lost with the pros

If the maze becomes too easy to navigate, the exploration and discovery disappears.
